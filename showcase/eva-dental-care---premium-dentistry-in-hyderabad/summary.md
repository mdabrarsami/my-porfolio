## What it does

The site presents the clinic, its doctors and its treatments, and turns visits into appointment requests. Patients book through a form with a date picker, or tap WhatsApp and call buttons that stay on screen. Treatment-specific landing pages target local searches such as dental implants or Invisalign in Narsingi. The live site is static HTML on Netlify; the repo also holds an earlier React + Vite version with a grand-opening countdown page.

## Highlights

- Live at evadental.in on Netlify, with clean URLs through redirects
- Four core pages plus treatment landing pages for implants, Invisalign, root canal, smile makeover, cosmetic dentistry, children's dentistry and maxillofacial surgery
- JSON-LD (schema.org) structured data on 11 pages, Open Graph and Twitter tags, sitemap and robots.txt
- Appointment form on Netlify Forms with a flatpickr date picker and SweetAlert2 confirmations
- Booking deep links (`#book`, `#appointment`) that scroll to the form and focus the first field
- Mobile nav menu and bottom action bar with call, WhatsApp and Book Appointment
- Google Tag Manager and a Google reviews widget
- Earlier React 19 + Vite version with an enquiry modal, countdown landing page and a Recharts analytics page

## Stack

HTML, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript, flatpickr, SweetAlert2, Netlify Forms, Google Tag Manager; earlier version in React 19, TypeScript, Vite, React Router, Recharts
