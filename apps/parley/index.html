Google Chat, as a desktop app

Parley is a Windows app for reading and sending your Google Chat messages. It connects to Google directly from your computer. There is no Parley server in between, and your messages are kept on your own machine.

Parley works with Google Workspace accounts that have Google Chat turned on. It is an independent project and is not made or endorsed by Google.

Your chats in one window

Direct messages, group chats and spaces, with unread counts, pinning, archiving and your own lists. Search across everything stored on your computer.

Messaging

Send, edit and delete messages, reply with a quote, react, mention people, attach files, record voice and video messages, and share a Google Meet link.

Stays out of the way

Runs in the system tray, shows Windows notifications you can reply to, puts an unread count on the taskbar, and can start with Windows.

Why it asks for Google access

Parley signs you in with Google and asks for permission to read and send your Chat messages, see the chats you are in and their members, and manage your read state and notification settings. It needs these to show and send your messages. It does nothing else with them.

Where your data lives

Messages and attachments are stored in a database on your computer. Your sign-in is kept in Windows Credential Manager. Signing out removes the stored chats. Nothing is uploaded to the developer.
