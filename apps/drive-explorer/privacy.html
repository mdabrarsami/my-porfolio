Privacy policy

Drive Explorer is a Windows desktop application that manages your Google Drive. It runs entirely on your own computer. There is no Drive Explorer server, so there is nowhere for your data to be collected to.

Drive Explorer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who runs this app

Drive Explorer is an independent project maintained by an individual developer. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Google LLC. Contact: mdabrarsami@gmail.com.

What the app accesses

When you sign in, you grant Drive Explorer these scopes. It uses them only for the features described here.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive
Lists, opens, uploads, downloads, moves, copies, renames and trashes files and folders in your Drive, and reads their names, sizes, types, owners and modification dates so the app can display them. This is the feature set of the app; it is used for nothing else.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email
Reads your email address, to label the signed-in account and to keep multiple accounts apart.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile
Reads your display name and profile picture, shown in the account switcher.

Where it is stored

Everything the app keeps is written to your own computer, under your Windows user profile.

File contents are not cached. A file is downloaded only when you ask for it, and it goes to the folder you choose.

Who it is shared with

  • Nobody. Your Google data is sent only between your computer and Google's own API endpoints.
  • There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting service and no advertising.
  • Nothing is sold, rented or transferred, and none of it is used to train any machine learning or AI model.
  • The developer cannot see your files, your account or your logs.

How long it is kept

The token and the local cache stay on your computer until you remove them. Signing out of an account inside the app revokes the token with Google and deletes that account's cached data at once.

How to revoke access and delete your data

  1. In Drive Explorer, open Settings, then Accounts, then Sign out. This revokes the token with Google and deletes the local record of that account.
  2. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page, whether or not the app is installed.
  3. To remove everything, uninstall the app and delete its data folder at %APPDATA%\Drive Explorer.

Children

Drive Explorer is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect any data from them.
