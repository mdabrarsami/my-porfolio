Capture, mark up and record your screen

Master Screenshot is a Windows app for screenshots, annotation and screen recording. It lives in the system tray and works from keyboard shortcuts. Everything you capture stays on your PC unless you choose to share it.

Signing in with Google is optional. It is only used to upload the captures you choose to your own Google Drive.

Screenshots

Capture an area, a window or the whole screen, including long scrolling pages. Pick colors, read text with Windows OCR, and pin a capture on top of other windows.

Annotation

Arrows, shapes, pen, highlighter, text, numbered steps, blur and mosaic. Every mark is a layer you can move, restyle or remove, and a capture can be reopened and edited again later.

Screen recording

Record to MP4 or GIF with microphone, system audio and a webcam bubble. Draw while recording, zoom on the mouse, trim afterwards, and optionally record meetings when a call starts.

Why it asks for Google access

If you sign in, the app asks for one permission: access to the files it creates in your Google Drive. It uses that to make a folder, upload the captures you choose, and share them by link. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

Where your data lives

Screenshots, recordings, settings and history are stored on your PC. Uploads go straight from your PC to your own Google Drive. Nothing is sent to the developer, and the app collects no analytics.
