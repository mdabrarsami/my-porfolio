Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Master Screenshot is a Windows app for screenshots, annotation and screen recording, built by Md Abrar Sami ("the developer"). This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends anywhere, and what control you have.

The short version: the app runs on your PC and has no servers of its own. The developer does not collect, sell or share your data.

What stays on your PC

Screenshots, recordings, captions, settings and capture history are saved on your computer, in the folders you choose and under your Windows user profile. They stay there unless you share them yourself. The app collects no analytics and sends nothing to the developer.

Google Drive (optional)

You can use the app without a Google account. If you choose to sign in with Google, the app asks for one permission: drive.file. With it, the app can:

  • create a folder in your Google Drive,
  • upload the screenshots and recordings you choose, or that you have set to upload automatically,
  • share those uploads by link, and delete an upload when you ask it to.

This permission covers only files the app itself creates. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

  • The app also receives the email address of the account you signed in with, to show which account is connected.
  • The sign-in token is stored on your PC only, encrypted for your Windows account.
  • Files go straight from your PC to your own Google Drive. Nothing passes through the developer or any other server.
  • Uploads are shared as "anyone with the link can view" when that setting is on. You can turn it off in Settings, under Sharing, to keep uploads private to your account.

Use of Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide the upload and sharing features you see in the app.
  • It is not transferred to anyone else, except to Google itself to perform the upload, to comply with the law, or with your explicit consent.
  • It is not used for advertising.
  • No person reads your data. The developer has no access to it.
  • It is not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Other ways of sharing that you set up

The app can also upload a capture to an address you enter yourself, copy it to a folder you choose, or hand it to your email program. These only happen when you configure and use them, and the capture goes only to the place you chose.

Speech and text recognition

Text extraction and automatic captions use recognition features built into Windows. Captions need Windows "Online speech recognition" to be turned on, in which case Windows may send audio to Microsoft under Microsoft's own privacy terms. The app itself sends that audio nowhere.

Removing your data and access

  • Sign out in Settings, under Sharing, to remove the token from your PC and revoke the app's access at Google.
  • Revoke access at any time from your Google Account, under Security, then "Your connections to third-party apps and services".
  • Delete captures from your PC like any other file. Files already uploaded stay in your Google Drive until you delete them there.

Security

The app connects to Google over encrypted connections, and your sign-in token is protected by Windows for your user account. Anyone who can use your Windows account can open the app and your captures, so protect your computer accordingly.

Children

Master Screenshot is not directed at children under 13.
